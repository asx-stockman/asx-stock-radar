Understanding your signals

BUY flags positive momentum and a confirming moving average. SELL flags technical weakness, not an instruction to short. WAIT means the rules don't show clear confirmation. Risk levels use historical volatility and liquidity.

Scores are experimental rule-based rankings, not probabilities, tested forecasts, or personalised investment advice. A high score does not mean a stock is likely to rise. Always verify prices and trading costs.
